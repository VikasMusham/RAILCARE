❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is RailMitra? +
RailMitra is a platform that connects passengers with verified railway station assistants. Our assistants help with luggage handling, seat escort, wheelchair assistance, and language support at railway stations across India.
How do I book an assistant? +
  1. Select your station and enter travel details
  2. Choose the services you need (Luggage, Seat Escort, etc.)
  3. Add passenger information
  4. Complete payment via UPI or Cash on Delivery
  5. An assistant will be assigned to you automatically
What services are available? +
  • Luggage Assistance: Help with carrying and managing your bags
  • Seat Escort: Guide to your seat/coach and boarding help
  • Wheelchair Assistance: Support for mobility-impaired passengers
  • Language Support: Communication help in regional languages
  • Round Trip: Assistance at both boarding and arrival stations
How much does it cost? +
Pricing depends on the services selected and number of luggage items. Base prices start from ₹49 for basic assistance. You can see the exact price breakdown in the booking summary before payment. We offer both online payment (UPI) and Cash on Delivery options.
What is the cancellation policy? +
  • Before assistant assigned: Full refund with no charges
  • After assistant assigned: ₹20 cancellation fee is deducted

Refunds for online payments are processed within 1-2 business days.

How do I track my assistant? +
Once an assistant is assigned, you can track your booking in "My Bookings". You'll see your assistant's name, phone number, and real-time status updates. You can also use the "Track Booking" button for detailed progress.
What are Start OTP and Completion OTP? +
  • Start OTP: Share this with your assistant when they arrive to begin the service
  • Completion OTP: Share this after the service is complete to confirm satisfaction

These OTPs ensure secure verification between you and the assistant.

What if I face an emergency? +
Use the 🚨 Emergency Help button available in active bookings. This will immediately alert your assistant and our support team. You can also contact your assistant directly using the phone number shown in your booking details.
How do I contact support? +
You can reach our support team through:
  • Email: support@railmitra.com
  • Phone: 1800-XXX-XXXX (Toll Free)
  • In-app feedback after completing a booking

Still have questions? Contact us at support@railmitra.com
